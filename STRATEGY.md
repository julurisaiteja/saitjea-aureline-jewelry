# Aureline — Studio Strategy

1. **Who is this for?** Gift buyers and self-purchase luxury clients seeking heirloom-weight everyday gold — not flash-sale jewelry.
2. **What makes it unique?** Candlelit atelier aesthetic: ink/ivory/gold, lookbook masonry, note-stack reviews — intimacy over sparkle spam.
3. **First 5 seconds?** Full-bleed craft film, monumental serif logotype, whisper-thin tracking — quiet luxury, not pink AI clone.
4. **Signature interaction?** **Atelier try-on** — 3D ring on velvet pedestal, yellow / rose / white gold, linked to piece grid and craft film.
5. **Primary journey?** Lookbook hero → atelier try-on → deep PDP → gift-wrap checkout (GLOW10) → success.
6. **Layout system?** Centered cinematic hero vs Formloom’s editorial split; hairline dividers, Cormorant + Outfit, dark panel surfaces.
7. **3D role?** Metal tone preview for rings; reduced-motion fallback image on canvas.
8. **What we avoid?** Sparkle spam, emoji, identical three-card review rows, shared section stacks with other showcase brands.
